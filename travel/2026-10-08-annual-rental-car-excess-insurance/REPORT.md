# Annual rental car excess insurance for a German resident (2026-10-08)

## Task

Find the best value annual rental car excess insurance (Selbstbeteiligungs-/Selbstbehalt-Versicherung)
for a 32-year-old German resident. It should cover the EU, UK and Switzerland, including Corfu (Greece).
Low-cost extras are fine but not essential.

## TL;DR

**Recommendation: Riverside Insurance Agency Malta, annual policy, "UK, Ireland and Europe" territory: €107.10/year**
(live quote on 2026-10-08 for a DE resident with 1 driver, cover starting 2026-10-15).

- Annual aggregate limit of **€10,000**, unlimited rentals of up to 31 days each.
- **Tyres, wheels, glass, underbody and roof are included**, as are keys, misfuelling, towing, admin fees and loss of use.
- "Europe" means EEA + Switzerland + UK + everything west of the Urals, including "islands in the Mediterranean". **Corfu, UK and CH are covered.**
- German residents are explicitly eligible (DE is in the residence dropdown; the policy is for EEA residents excluding UK).
- Underwritten by Fortegra Europe Insurance Company SE, regulated by the Malta FSA. It is the EU arm of UK broker Questor.
- **Worldwide is only €114.24**, €7 more. Take it if there is any chance of a non-European rental within the year.

Runner-up among German providers: **Carassure Mietwagen Europa, €149.90/year**. It is more expensive and caps
payouts at **€1,500 per claim**, which is below many rental excesses. It allows longer rentals (60 days).

## Riverside Malta details (from policy wording `RIAML_MTAnnual_Policy_EN_28032024_V1.2`)

- **Eligibility:** aged 21 or over at purchase; permanent resident in the EEA (not UK, CI, IoM or Gibraltar); valid licence.
- **Vehicles:** up to 9 seats, value up to €100,000 and no more than 20 years old. Excludes vans, motorhomes, off-road vehicles,
  motorbikes and commercial vehicles. Motorhomes have a separate product.
- **Covered:** excess for accidental and malicious damage, weather, fire, and animal collisions. Its "Loss Damage Waiver" section
  covers tyres, wheels, rims, glass (including lights and sunroof), undercarriage and roof. Also covers admin charges, towing,
  misfuelling, keys and locks, loss of use, curtailment, drop-off charges and some personal effects.
- **Theft:** section 3 lists "Theft or attempted theft *if you choose to purchase this cover*". No separate theft extra appeared
  in the DE quote, and the LDW definition includes theft. **Ask them in writing before buying** whether theft excess is included.
- **Home-country rentals** (Germany) are included.
- **Additional drivers:** up to 5 are covered if they are on the rental agreement and the policyholder is the lead
  renter. Add "Dual Lead Driver" (+€53.55) if a partner will rent in their own name.
- **Optional extras offered in the quote:** Battery Failure €16.06, Extended Rental to 62 days €53.55,
  Dual Lead Driver €53.55. On Worldwide there is also CDW (sum insured €75k) at €57.12. None is needed for a typical EU holiday.
- **Reimbursement model:** you pay the rental company first, then claim online via Orchard Administration
  within one calendar month of the incident. They pay within 5 working days once all documents are in.
- **Main exclusions:** breach of the rental agreement, driving off public roads, unnamed drivers, interior damage
  (except from a collision), alcohol or drugs, and anything recoverable from the renter's other insurance or third parties.
- **Annual aggregate:** once €10,000 is used up, the policy ends.

## Practical notes for Corfu and Greece

- Greek rental contracts often exclude damage to tyres, glass, underbody and on ferries, even with the desk's "full insurance".
  Riverside covers the first three, but **not damage arising from a breach of contract**. If the contract forbids ferries or
  unpaved roads, damage there is not covered.
- **Off-road exclusion:** some Corfu beach access roads are unpaved tracks. Damage there may count as "not a public road".
- Still take the rental's basic CDW/TP (with excess). Excess insurance only covers the excess, not the car itself.
  Decline the desk's "super CDW" / zero-excess upsell.
- Pay with a real credit card. The desk will block the excess as a deposit.
- Photograph or film the car, including underbody, rims and windscreen, at pickup and return.

## Is annual worth it?

Rental desks charge about €15–30/day to reduce the excess to zero. Single-trip excess policies cost about €5–8/day.
At €107/year, the annual policy pays off from about **4–7 rental days per year** compared with the desk, or about 15–20 days
compared with daily excess policies. For fewer rental days, a Riverside daily policy (from €5.19/day) or a German single-trip
product (HanseMerkur up to €2,500, ERGO from €7.90/day) is cheaper.
